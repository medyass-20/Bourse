# Bourse X 

### AI-Powered Stock Analysis & Portfolio Optimization

**Bourse X** is a FinTech project that aims to develop an intelligent financial decision-support platform by combining **Machine Learning, stock market analysis, and portfolio optimization**.

The project focuses on analyzing US financial markets, identifying potentially outperforming stocks, and optimizing investment capital allocation while minimizing estimated portfolio risk.

Developed by a team of six Computer Science Engineering students, QuantPilot AI brings together Artificial Intelligence, Data Engineering, Quantitative Finance, and Software Engineering.

## Project Objectives

The platform aims to address three fundamental questions:

1. **Stock Analysis & Prediction:** Which stocks are most likely to outperform others in the market?
2. **Portfolio Optimization:** How should investment capital be distributed among selected stocks to minimize estimated risk?
3. **Strategy Evaluation:** How would the proposed investment strategy have performed on historical market data?

The ultimate goal is to transform financial data into actionable analytical insights through an integrated and interactive platform.

## How It Works

QuantPilot AI is designed around two complementary engines:

### 1. AI-Powered Stock Selection

A Machine Learning model analyzes historical stock market data and financial indicators to identify stocks with the highest estimated probability of relative outperformance.

Rather than predicting exact future stock prices, the model focuses on **ranking stocks according to their potential performance relative to other stocks**.

### 2. Intelligent Portfolio Optimization

Using the stocks selected by the AI model, a portfolio optimization engine based on **Modern Portfolio Theory (Markowitz)** determines how to distribute investment capital.

The objective is to minimize estimated portfolio risk while respecting allocation and diversification constraints.

### 3. Historical Strategy Evaluation

A backtesting component evaluates the combined stock selection and portfolio optimization strategy using historical market data.

This allows the project to analyze historical performance, assess financial risk, and compare the proposed strategy against market benchmarks.

## Project Vision

QuantPilot AI aims to demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence and quantitative financial methods can work together to support more structured, data-driven investment decisions.

The project emphasizes:

- Data-driven financial analysis
- Machine Learning for stock ranking
- Risk-aware capital allocation
- Historical strategy evaluation
- Modular and maintainable software architecture
